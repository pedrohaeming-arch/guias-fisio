Frequently asked questions

Isn't this the same as searching online?

The information is out there, scattered. What is not out there is the same information in the right order, with its source alongside, and without one page contradicting the next. That is why the guide is short: the work of separating what holds up from what does not has already been done.

I don't have time to read.

That is exactly why the guide is in pocket format. If you only have ten minutes, read the "What to do" block and the routine at the end — that is enough to start today.

How do I receive the guide?

As soon as your payment clears, you get an email with access to the PDF. By card it arrives immediately; slower payment methods can take up to 2 business days.

Can I read it on my phone?

Yes. The pocket format exists precisely so it sits comfortably on a phone screen, and the file prints well too.

Does the guide replace seeing a physiotherapist?

No, and it doesn't try to. It's educational material. If your pain is severe, persistent, or comes with other symptoms, get an in-person assessment — the guide says so itself.

Where does the information come from?

Every myth and every figure in the guide names the document — a guideline, a study or an institution, with the year. If the source does not confirm it, it does not go in the guide.

What if I don't like it?

You have 7 days to request a full refund through the payment platform. Nothing to explain.

Will you remember what to do tomorrow?

That's what the guide is for. Pick yours and start today, at your own pace.
